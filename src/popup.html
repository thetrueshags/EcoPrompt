How we estimate Method & limits

All figures are illustrative estimates, not measured consumption. We estimate tokens from the visible text of new prompts and replies. Tokens are small pieces of text; these counts are a rough proxy, not provider billing counts.

The model applies the same illustrative assumptions to every platform. It does not detect your model, data center, or electricity source.

These coefficients are product assumptions, not verified averages. Water is an illustrative cooling-water scenario. Carbon is energy × assumed grid intensity. Neither captures the full lifecycle footprint.

Actual use varies with model, response length, reasoning, hardware, cooling, and location. Hidden reasoning, reused conversation context, images, audio, video, tools, training, and device energy are outside this estimate.

Research context: Epoch AI ↗ · Google’s inference study ↗. These studies do not validate our per-token coefficients.

Your data Private by default

Only numeric usage totals, platform names, dates, and tracking settings are saved locally. Chat text is processed in memory and is never stored or sent by EcoPrompt. No accounts, analytics, or external requests.

Daily history is kept for 30 local calendar days; all-time totals remain until you clear them. Refresh existing AI tabs after installing or updating.
